Research Interests

My research focuses on multimodal AI—how we can build models that perceive, ground, and reason over long, complex procedures with different modalities involved. I develop benchmarks that stress advanced reasoning, grounding frameworks that align language with space-time, and representation learning methods that scale robustly.

  • Video Understanding / Reasoning Benchmarks: VEBEnch(CVPR Findings 2026), SciVideoBench(ICML 2026), GroundMoRe(CVPR 2025), Sports-QA(IJCV 2026)
    Rigorous evaluations of multimodal reasoning in scientific and real-world domains.
  • Video Spatial / Temporal Grounding Frameworks: Seq2Time(CVPR 2025), MoRA(CVPR 2025)
    Mapping language to precise space-time segments with sequence-level localization.
  • Representation Learning: BEAR(ICCV 2023), Inadequate Pre-training(ICCV 2023), OGM-GE(CVPR 2022), MoPRL(TCSVT 2022)
    Foundation features and training recipes for robust visual perception and reasoning.
